## Why these six files exist

The [v1.85_EN.md](v1.85_EN.md) document already gives an extensive English technical design and a numbered crosswalk for previous versions, but some historical chapters in [v1.85_ZH-TW.md](v1.85_ZH-TW.md) contain **more explanatory paragraphs, decision rationale, tabulated interfaces, historical status changes, exact test criteria and deferred limits** than that crosswalk alone. The following English files fill those gaps as detailed historical translations. They should be read in conjunction with the latest **v1.85 effective specification**, which supersedes older provisional statements.

## Authority and accuracy rules

1. **Historical, not automatically current.** A statement such as “Q01 death semantics still pending” was correct as a record in v1.81/v1.82 but later v1.83 approved residents' own interpretations. A v1.84 report that 24 DPB names were missing was superseded when v1.85 located all 24 historical candidates. Similarly, v1.79 had 149 formal R requirements whereas v1.80 adopted 21 more, totaling **170**.
2. **Approval is not implementation.** `APPROVED` describes human approval of a design direction. `PAPER_CLOSED` is a documentation decision; `ENGINEERING_CANDIDATE` is not an approved numerical implementation; `NOT_RUN` means that a case has not been executed. No actual full 24-DPB simulation, agent program or end-to-end magical-world validation is presented.
3. **Translations are faithful detailed technical renditions, not certified literal equivalence.** The six files translate the source chapters' decisions, numbered registers, tables, functional clauses, tests and boundary conditions. Sentence grouping and technical English terminology may differ from the Chinese paragraphs. The preserved Chinese primary-language cumulative source remains the authority when resolving ambiguous phrasing, together with the later approved v1.85 effective text.
4. **Original private master not released.** The historical translations cover the publicly available v1.85 cumulative chapters (v1.79–v1.84), not the unavailable earlier hundreds-page original source or private person-to-person messages. R-01–R-149 are known via public issue/source crosswalk, not represented as the full original line-by-line mother document.
5. **Privacy and IP boundaries remain.** No private diary, identity data, credentials, conversation transcripts, protected third-party images or other undisclosed source artifacts have been newly included. The published design of privacy/security mechanisms is distinct from private resident content.
6. **P-02 remains.** These are translations and documentation-only changes, not authorization to build or deploy agent software or commit any private source archive.
