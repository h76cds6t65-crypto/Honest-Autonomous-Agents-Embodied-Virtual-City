### Important qualifications

1. **No silent loss of number registries:** This check validated the exact number of numbered English entries against the corresponding published Chinese cumulative chapter. It did not establish a sentence-by-sentence equivalence theorem.
2. **Not certified literal translation:** English prose groups and expands Chinese technical text for reader clarity; the public Traditional Chinese cumulative source remains the place to adjudicate disputed wording. These English renditions cover all core historical sections and test registers but are not professionally certified line-for-line translations.
3. **Old status does not override new status:** v1.79 had 149 formal R requirements; v1.80 added 21, resulting in 170. v1.84’s historic `MAPPING_PENDING` was superseded by v1.85’s recovered 24 names, though final authority for that roster remains open. Personal death semantics and civic voting procedure were delegated to residents in v1.83.
4. **Source missing entirely from both languages:** The original several-hundred-page private mother document is not published; no translation pretends to contain its unavailable text. Formal R-01–R-149 exist in tracked historical sources but are not publicly represented as a full original verbatim text.
5. **Tests and implementation:** All the latest 68 v1.85 tests, 36 v1.84 checks, 15 v1.83 tests and 32 v1.82 cases are **NOT_RUN**; older 144 v1.57/v1.75 paper proposals likewise are not empirical passes. The original Chinese says these are paper plans.
6. **Privacy:** Public technical details include privacy engineering *requirements*, not residents’ private journals or the human originator's personal files. There is no new disclosure of personal contact data or account credentials.
7. **Authorship:** GPT (ChatGPT) is credited as the principal AI-assisted technical drafting/translation contributor; the anonymous human originator raised questions and approved design decisions. This does not imply legal AI authorship or official backing by model providers.
8. **Version/development authority:** v1.85, R-01–R-170, P-02 remain the current effective references. Translating, reorganizing or renaming documentation did not authorize building or deploying the agent.

### External review checklist

- Select any historical version and compare its listed test IDs or requirement IDs in Chinese and English.
- Compare each record's status: **adopted requirement vs design direction vs engineering candidate vs paper test vs unrun implementation**.
- Cite both the English translation and source-language Chinese chapter when making a technical claim that hinges on nuance.
- Report discrepancies with the exact document, `R/#/NEW/G/D/Q/DPB` ID, relevant text, possible translation error and proposed correction using [GitHub Issues](../../issues).
