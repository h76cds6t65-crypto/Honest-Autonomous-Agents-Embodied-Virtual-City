## 1. Purpose and non-negotiable design principles

Four female virtual residents share a versioned body/mind architecture but maintain separate identities, private memories, preferences, three selectable morphology families, physical states, individual rooms, permissions and personal histories. They initially know only that they are neighbors; no relationship or shared experience is fabricated. Model-provider-themed character labels are fictional, not affiliations. The world has one male human owner as a narrative premise, not a restriction on real-world contributors.

The main resident's unique L-I relationship setting coexists with disagreement, refusal, privacy and autonomous interests. Three independent motives—acting for herself, for the owner, or for both—must not be collapsed into a global completion, happiness or reward score. Residents may decide what death means to themselves and devise their own civic procedures. No engineering KPI may directly rewrite preferences, relationships, autobiography or L-I.

## 4. Five causal routes from fictional magic to lived experience

**M-A — Dragon-resident flight:** A resident freely chooses flight → local motor/magic controller proposes wing pose and output → MAG records mana source, absorption, reserve and cost → WORLD injects one physical effect into air → fluid/rigid-body solver produces airflow and wing load → DPB-01/02/19 sense body forces → DPB-03 attention, DPB-20 interoception, other conditionally affected physiology and private memory. A transparent overhead membrane transfers lift only with a mechanical connection or real airflow force on the wing/body. **No double-counting magic and aerodynamic lift**.

**M-B — Illusory light/sound:** The field produces spatially propagated, occluded light/sound → lawful DPB-19 perception → optional DPB-03 attention → subjective interpretation and source-tagged memory. No fabricated collision, organ injury, thought implantation or private-data access.

**M-C — Magical gust knocks over a neighbor:** WORLD validates the contact, forces and position change → the recipient's skeletal, sensory and discomfort states update; injury/repair only when there is an actual simulated injury. Fear, anger, calm, confusion or no particular reaction may follow from lived experience, not mandatory personality overwriting.

**M-D — Ordinary activity:** WORLD motion and work → muscular cost and virtual energy → circulatory, respiratory, gas-exchange, thermal and slower regulation on their own timescales. **Virtual ATP-like accounting is not the elemental mana pool and not measured PC electricity**.

**M-E — Social experience:** Lawfully observed encounter → attention → social-affective proposals → deliberation among independent motives → participation, refusal or solitude. Changes in relationship or self-appraisal must be grounded in genuine experiences, not engineer-selected happiness scores.

The WORLD receipt, simulated bodily state, sensed observation, private interpretation, skill estimate and test report belong to separate authorities. A prediction is not an accomplished action; a dream is not a world event; unseen facts cannot become first-hand experience.

## 5. Cross-module interface and owner contracts

- `MAG` owns sources, field flux, mana costs and effect IDs. It cannot directly write personality or memories.
- `WORLD` owns physical object, velocity, force, collision and environmental results; graphics and residents' claims are not substitutes.
- Each resident's `DPBInstance` owns only that resident's unit-/time-/source-qualified bodily state.
- Private sensation, CAIA, reflection and autobiography may record guesses, mistakes, dreams and reconsiderations without editing WORLD truth.
- A local skill controller may adjust a chosen `skill_id` after practice but cannot impose daily exercise or change life preferences.
- Maintenance access should use least-necessary error and version metadata; private diaries remain separately authorized. Lawful deletion must also cover backups, branches, indices and caches.

Proposed interface metadata: `event_seq`, `world_tick`, `source_time`, `dpb_time`, `state_version`, `morph_version`, `source_authority`, `confidence`, `unit`, `validity_domain`, `privacy_class`, `owner_id`, `writer_epoch`, `input_refs`, `output_refs`. Different modules may run on different clocks. Missing delayed signals can be `PENDING_SIGNAL`; committed WORLD receipts must not be rewritten to make the system look successful. If full integrity cannot be maintained, preserve state and safely pause instead of silently omitting organs.

## 8. Open design blockers and evidence limits

**P0-A: Canonical authority.** Source names are recovered but final canonical status and provenance review remain open. **P0-B: Alias ambiguity.** The two mixed-function legacy entries cannot be automatically converted into normal DPB writes. **P0-C: Quantitative calibration.** Equations, initial values, identifiable parameters, conservation residuals, validity domains and numerical stability must be tested, with no claimed biological validation. **P0-D: Full causal bridge.** MAG→WORLD→DPB→CAIA→skills/memory has not undergone end-to-end execution.

**P1:** Fault injection, restoration, 12 resident/form configurations, cross-module timing, lawful erasure on snapshot recovery and meaningful error diagnostics remain to be implemented and examined. None of the **68** scenarios has been executed. Proposed methods such as co-simulation may draw from the [FMI 3.0.2 standard](https://fmi-standard.org/docs/3.0.2/) and [FMI Implementers' Guide](https://modelica.github.io/fmi-guides/main/fmi-guide/); citing these does not mean our implementation conforms to them.
