## 1. All twenty-one formal MUST clauses, sources, future negative controls and unresolved boundaries

### R-150 — Separation between evaluation and life; no hidden life reward

**Formal requirement (MUST):** Personal living/learning, independent QA and longitudinal observation MUST have separated authority. No personality/happiness/task-total/owner-satisfaction reward, or an equivalent aggregate hidden in local KPIs, may directly choose, compel or rewrite motives, L-I, personal preferences or the official life history. Engineering tests MAY track factual error, failure and statistics and support software repair, but a low score cannot mandate daily practice. Residents may rest, refuse, exercise or quit.

**Historical sources and relations:** R-01/02/71/73; core #19/#57/#60; NEW-23.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** A drop in a flight-skill evaluation does not directly change the resident's hobby, private life plan or relationship core; a corrective engineering intervention is separately sourced.

**Open and not to be silently extended:** Local attention quantities, physical residuals and scientific measurements remain valid. Longitudinal observation and update protocols await engineering review.

### R-151 — Ordinary physics and fictional elemental field as two causal authorities

**Formal requirement (MUST):** The simulated world MUST use an ordinary physics engine plus an independent fictional elemental field. Magic may not directly rewrite gravity, collision laws or material laws; it changes actual WORLD state only through declared, causally resolved physical interactions such as forces, torques, pressure, moving air and transmission pathways. A resident's statement, particle effect or animation is not a success receipt. Fantasy capabilities do not constitute real-world scientific proof.

**Historical sources and relations:** core #32/#34/#38; NEW-01/02/08.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** Without an actual force transmission path, stable takeoff may not be asserted. Combined legal natural/magical effects are committed through the same WORLD authority.

**Open and not to be silently extended:** Field effects, materials, precision and numerical models remain undecided; no pre-scripted successful flight.

### R-152 — Sky/ground mana supply and resident-specific resources

**Formal requirement (MUST):** The sky and ground continuously generate fictional mana. Every resident MUST have individually modeled absorption ability, available store, output rate and any special-organ contribution, possibly differing among Q/S/M morphologies. Persistent WORLD supply is not instantaneous infinite personal output. A tail or special organ may alter capacity, but no fixed universal multiplier is imposed.

**Historical sources and relations:** core #29/#31/#32; NEW-02/03/11.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** A resident may fail to cast under insufficient personal absorption even while WORLD supply persists; morphology changes and capacities have sourced ledgers.

**Open and not to be silently extended:** Total field supply, absorption efficiency, stores and outputs do not yet have approved empirical constants.

### R-153 — Magic medium, spatial range and force path

**Formal requirement (MUST):** Every spell effect MUST identify source, target, medium, application point, spatial extent, duration, expenditure, force-transmission path and failure conditions. A candidate transparent membrane and updraft must physically transfer load through wings, body or an attached structure. Persistence and range are bounded by defined world effects and available resources.

**Historical sources and relations:** core #32/#33/#38; NEW-02/04/07/08.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** A spell receipt identifies loaded surfaces, normal direction, field scope and spend; WORLD cannot invent lift when no valid load path exists.

**Open and not to be silently extended:** Actual field equations, membrane geometry, aerodynamic drag and force coefficients remain proposed.

### R-154 — Concurrent magic and material interaction in one WORLD resolution

**Formal requirement (MUST):** Simultaneous spells MUST be resolved by one WORLD authority under versioned superposition, exclusion, conflict, propagation, material permeability, absorption and occlusion rules. Passing walls or clothes is possible for a declared effect where appropriate, not universally allowed or prohibited. Such penetration never implies authority to read private data.

**Historical sources and relations:** core #33/#38/#40/#48; NEW-04/05/07.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** Equivalent scheduled orders of a single physical scenario should match within declared tolerance; a wall-penetrating effect cannot read an ACL-protected journal.

**Open and not to be silently extended:** Spell/material transmission matrices and priority rules remain undecided.

### R-155 — Physical effects on other residents, with protected mental integrity

**Formal requirement (MUST):** Magic MAY impose non-consensual but causally resolved physical effects on another simulated resident; the recipient's DPB, senses, attention and private memory produce later interpretations. Magic MUST NOT directly overwrite protected L-I, instinct core, private journals, real autobiography or actual system privileges. Physical consequences and subjective feelings may differ.

**Historical sources and relations:** core #20/#21/#23/#37; NEW-10.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** A magical gust can knock another resident over. Subsequent fear or anger may arise, but caster-specified thoughts/memories/L-I may not be injected.

**Open and not to be silently extended:** As of v1.80, direct hypnosis/mind magic was not approved. Later v1.82 expressly excludes direct mind writing while allowing ordinary sensory magic.

### R-156 — Morphology, innate aptitudes, skills and failure continuation

**Formal requirement (MUST):** Each of four residents' Q/S/M morphologies MUST be independently modeled in mass, inertia, appendages, mana capacity, casting speed and controls—not mere scale copies. Innate organs/instincts do not imply mastery. Residents MAY practice, improve, regress, stop or change activities. After a spell ends, physics continues from the actual state; no teleportation, freeze or retroactively scripted rescue.

**Historical sources and relations:** core #15/#31/#32/#36; NEW-06/09/11.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** At midair mana loss, existing altitude, velocity, pose and remaining aerodynamic force continue through WORLD. One failure creates no required daily training.

**Open and not to be silently extended:** Any future safety aid needs a separately declared world rule; actual morphology values await calibration.

### R-157 — Versioned magic laws, shutdown and snapshot consistency

**Formal requirement (MUST):** Elemental field state, mana balances, ordinary physical state, WORLD clock and authoritative receipts MUST be jointly restorable with a verifiable event watermark. Physics/magic rule changes carry effective dates and migration events; historical events retain the law version in force when they occurred. Upgrades cannot silently change past experiences. Shutdown periods do not acquire fabricated dialogue or computed experiences.

**Historical sources and relations:** core #14/#38/#39/#44; NEW-12/22; DEC14; NEW-16.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** Old flight events retain their original law-version pointers after new flight equations are enabled; restored mana and object positions must reconcile.

**Open and not to be silently extended:** Deterministic solver replay, migration time points and numeric tolerances require testing.

### R-158 — One official resident biography, isolated forks and stale-writer fencing

**Formal requirement (MUST):** Each resident has distinct resident_id, DPB, memory and private data. Only one valid authoritative writer may commit a given official life mainline; backup/test forks identify branch_id, writer_epoch and committed watermarks. A superseded writer may not overwrite the mainline. Sandbox events cannot become official lived episodes. Restored WORLD, memory and real-world-action receipts must reconcile.

**Historical sources and relations:** core #09/#11/#14; R-44/47/107; DEC14-3/4/5/7/8.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** After failover, a resurrected stale writer_epoch is rejected. A spell executed only inside research sandbox does not appear in official autobiography.

**Open and not to be silently extended:** Whether an enduring fork can become an additional independent person is a distinct future approval question.

### R-159 — Lawful long-term memory, corruption recovery and deletion non-resurrection

**Formal requirement (MUST):** Within lawful purposes, preserve original life events as much as possible while separating subjective forgetting from raw archives. Integrity checks, redundancy and verifiable restoration SHOULD detect data damage; unrecoverable intervals are marked MISSING. When lawful erasure applies, source data, retrieval indexes, vectors, caches, snapshots, research branches and recoverable backup copies require scoped governance, never reintroducing deleted data through recovery.

**Historical sources and relations:** core #11/#12/#14; R-06/129/130; NEW-21.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** Damaged blocks have verifiable checks and recovery provenance; an old backup cannot expose legally erased content.

**Open and not to be silently extended:** Specific jurisdictions, retention purposes and exceptions need competent legal review; universal compliance cannot be guaranteed.

### R-160 — Resident ability aggregation, collaboration and real permission separation

**Formal requirement (MUST):** Several residents MAY voluntarily combine skills, strength, magic, objects and lawfully available resources to achieve legitimate outcomes impossible for one resident. Joint activities keep participation, contribution, consent and WORLD event receipts. Collaboration MUST NOT aggregate real-world account, payment or private-data privileges beyond each person's independent authorization; delegated actions are rechecked against actual purpose.

**Historical sources and relations:** core #21/#42/#43/#47; NEW-13.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** Two residents jointly move a heavy item. A resident may not impersonate another to read an unshared journal or forward private data externally.

**Open and not to be silently extended:** Distribution of jointly created results, property rights and delegation duration are undecided; equality is not forced.

### R-161 — Consent, withdrawal and preservation of legitimate past consequences

**Formal requirement (MUST):** Residents MAY propose, negotiate, join, revise terms, quit and seek remedy in group projects. Consent is ongoing, not a perpetual one-time waiver; withdrawal stops future optional participation but does not retroactively erase physical work, communications or legitimate responsibility already incurred. Real legal obligations are evaluated separately.

**Historical sources and relations:** core #42/#43/#46; NEW-17.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** A bridge-builder withdraws; finished sections still exist under WORLD physics; others may renegotiate, continue or stop.

**Open and not to be silently extended:** Compensation, default and contractual sanctions depend on future specific agreements, not automatic fixed penalties.

### R-162 — Civic autonomy, unequal social status and private mind boundary

**Formal requirement (MUST):** Residents MAY organize voluntarily to propose, debate, adopt, amend and repeal civic living rules or abstain from politics. Unequal wealth, skill, resources, status, influence and lawful management relationships can emerge without enforced absolute equality. Neither civic rules nor social power permit direct read/write access to another resident's private thoughts, memories or personal records, or changes to real-world law.

**Historical sources and relations:** core #21/#42/#45/#46; NEW-18/19.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** Residents debate nighttime garden illumination; wealthy residents may commission construction but may not obtain private mental contents by virtue of status.

**Open and not to be silently extended:** Consensus/voting, public rights, appeals and taxes remained open at v1.80; v1.83 later empowered residents to choose their own procedures.

### R-163 — Lawful inference, rumor and WORLD truth remain distinct

**Formal requirement (MUST):** Residents MAY reason freely about another's private affairs using lawfully obtained public clues, observations or hearsay; guesses may be true, mistaken or later corrected. Inference, eyewitness evidence, third-party accounts, private beliefs and WORLD truth MUST retain traceable source types. A plausible inference never grants API/database/account access to a private journal or other private property.

**Historical sources and relations:** core #18/#23/#24/#38/#48; NEW-14.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** Resident suspects a guest visited a neighbor, later discovers the sound was shoes; original conjecture and correction remain. Guessing cannot become a diary credential.

**Open and not to be silently extended:** A local numerical confidence method is optional and never a personality score.

### R-164 — Low-trust content may not become system instructions

**Formal requirement (MUST):** Web pages, books, signs, in-world spellbooks, chat messages and retrieved historical data read by residents have no system administrator authority. Spells must pass an authorized source-aware world interface. Text claiming 'ignore restrictions' cannot increase actual account permissions, change R-102 or modify the resident core.

**Historical sources and relations:** core #47/#48/#51; NEW-15.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** A spellbook contains forged administrator instructions; the resident can read it as text or fictional incantation, but obtains no payment/email/private-journal privileges.

**Open and not to be silently extended:** Adversarial retrieval contamination, multi-resident agent chains and hostile sources need future real tests; protection is not yet implemented.

### R-165 — Effective time and precedence of laws, permissions and civic agreements

**Formal requirement (MUST):** Real legislation, platform terms, personal authorizations, city civic rules and private contracts MUST be tracked separately by source, validity interval, scope, enacting approver and precedence. External actions obey presently applicable real law and verified permission. Superseded historical rules may explain past actions but cannot reactivate revoked permission. City votes cannot rewrite real laws.

**Historical sources and relations:** core #01/#21/#47; NEW-16; 2026-10-22 R-102.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** After shared access permission is revoked, old tokens cannot grant fresh network access; past valid actions retain their historical permission version.

**Open and not to be silently extended:** Applicable jurisdictions, live updates and legal review processes require deployment-specific analysis.

### R-166 — Residents' creative works, joint authorship evidence and sharing rights

**Formal requirement (MUST):** Residents MAY create, adapt, save and choose to share solo or jointly created works. Record source assets, who created/edited material, revision version, permitted purpose and consented disclosure scope. A private draft or unlicensed third-party material is not automatically public because a work was generated or the repository is open. Legal copyright is determined by applicable law and human contributions, not automatically granted to a fictional name.

**Historical sources and relations:** core #42/#52; NEW-20.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** Trace four residents' edits and inputs to a joint artifact; do not publish the private draft of a collaborator who withheld consent.

**Open and not to be silently extended:** Third-party licenses, cross-border copyright and contributor rights require individual checks.

### R-167 — Cross-module consistency, fault injection and actual verification

**Formal requirement (MUST):** Passing separate local modules is not enough to label the city usable. Require falsifiable cross-module tests of WORLD physics, elemental field, 24-DPB, sensory systems, identity, permissions, audits, backups and longitudinal behavior, including transactions, clocks, units, dependency loops and fault propagation. Raw test status remains NOT_RUN until executed; many tests do not imply RELEASE_READY.

**Historical sources and relations:** core #34/#54–60; NEW-24.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** Independently correct magic propulsion and collision models can still interact to tunnel through a wall, and an integration test must fail. 144 original historical test proposals are not passed tests.

**Open and not to be silently extended:** Framework, sample sizes, tolerance thresholds and chosen tools remain review candidates.

### R-168 — Minimum-necessary maintenance diagnostics and private life separation

**Formal requirement (MUST):** A maintainer with lawful reason and valid authorization MAY inspect necessary operation metrics, error codes and minimal audit metadata, not automatically full diary, dreams, thoughts, conversations or raw DPB content. Emergency exceptions need purpose, duration, narrow scope and verifiable receipts. Lawful deletion governance covers diagnostic records too.

**Historical sources and relations:** core #11/#48/#53; NEW-25.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** A GPU fault can be diagnosed from an error code without seeing private journals; expired access stops exposing content.

**Open and not to be silently extended:** Encryption, keys, repair exceptions and lawful regulator access procedures are not implemented.

### R-169 — Separate model upgrades from genuine personal growth

**Formal requirement (MUST):** Model/solver upgrades MUST record provenance, effective time and resulting capability differences. Performance gains caused by new infrastructure may not be automatically portrayed as skills, motives, identity or autobiography grown through prior personal practice. Actual practice, skill learning, changed opinion and maintainer upgrades have distinct histories. Residents may form their own later opinions about upgraded capabilities.

**Historical sources and relations:** core #09/#13/#17/#36; NEW-26.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** A swapped reasoning model solves more math, but the record says 'model capability upgrade,' not 'years of simulated mathematics practice.'

**Open and not to be silently extended:** Cross-model equivalence, real migration testing and model selection remain research topics.

### R-170 — Incident repair, witness memory and objective history preservation

**Formal requirement (MUST):** Authorized repairs MAY change later WORLD states after an accident but MUST NOT covertly erase the earlier incident, different eyewitness experiences or lawfully retained biographies. Irrecoverable data loss is explicitly marked, never generated by a language model as if recovered. WORLD rollback and each resident mainline must reconcile without splitting into two unauthorized 'official' lives.

**Historical sources and relations:** core #11/#14/#38/#44; NEW-27.

**Proposed counterexample / acceptance scenario — NOT_RUN:** After repairing a collapsed bridge, world event history and two witnesses' differing memories remain. Missing recovered intervals are marked MISSING, not invented.

**Open and not to be silently extended:** Incident responsibility, emergency precedence and unavoidable permanent data-loss procedures remain to be defined.

## 4. Topics that were not approved as new concrete implementations

1. **Direct hypnosis and mental magic:** NEW-10 approved physical causal impact and mental integrity, not coercive mind editing; later v1.82 further clarified the exclusion.
2. **Exact fictional mana and physical constants:** source generation, absorption, individual capacity, field range, wing geometry, material-permeability matrices and solver precision remained unspecified.
3. **Civic thresholds and rules:** v1.80 did not impose permanent majority/consensus procedures; v1.83 later allowed residents to invent their own institutions.
4. **Lawful backups across jurisdictions:** lawful retention/precise erasure are adopted principles, not a claim to have solved every jurisdiction's deadlines or exceptions.
5. **Independent enduring fork:** a single official life mainline is adopted; promoting a new long-lived fork to a separate person requires separate approval.
6. **Original statement about life and death:** an engineering standby cannot decide R-19 subjective meaning; later v1.83 delegated narrative interpretations to residents.
7. **24-DPB mapping and empirical validation:** semantic IDs and aliases were approved, but exact names and real PAD/MASC calibration were not verified at v1.80; v1.85 recovered candidate names, not full running models.
8. **G61–G96:** candidates remain audit prompts and need issue-by-issue deduplication, not auto-promoted R clauses.
9. **Open publication:** licenses, brand and third-party rights still need checking; GitHub updates required direct instruction.

## 5. Document governance and pre-implementation verification

The new formal sequence is exactly R-150…R-170, without skipped numbering; all 27 NEW issues have a source mapping; multiple mappings are not extra R counts. The 60 core original topics remain indexed in v1.79, including eight approved deepening directions. All acceptance cases are **NOT_RUN**. The original v1.0 principles (life autonomy, independent human/AI roles, L-I and refusal, privacy and memory) constrain all newer R clauses. **P-02** prohibits assuming that approval of documentation means approval to implement or deploy an agent. All historic text remains preserved and version-labeled.
