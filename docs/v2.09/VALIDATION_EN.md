## 2. Numerically checkable reduced results

- 12 independently integrated cases: Q/S/M × rest24, stress72, fluid24, feedback_off24.
- Common initial WORLD: water 1000kg, Na/K/Cl 300mol each, chemical energy 10^10J.
- Baseline ATP power hypotheses Q/S/M 4.1/19.4/24.8 W.
- Stress interval: rest 0–1h, multiplier 1.30 at 1–9h, recovery 9–72h, an actual 28,800-second load.
- Fluid perturbation: transfer 5% of interstitial-water stock into WORLD water, with no spontaneous mass deletion.
- SciPy BDF settings: rtol=2×10^-8, atol=1×10^-9, maximum step 900s.
- Maximum numerical total-ledger residuals in the specified 12 runs: water about **5.68×10^-13kg**, energy about **1.91×10^-5J**, paired Na/K/Cl about **6.82×10^-13mol**.
- Ablating individual flow/renal/metabolic actuators changes six-hour responses, but **health benefit** is not established.

These values are arithmetic evidence for a deliberately restricted experiment, not physiology of real or imaginary species confirmed by observation.
