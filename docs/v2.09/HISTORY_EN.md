## History must not override current evidence

This is a de-identified **technical history digest**, not a lossless publication of confidential Word files or multigeneration ZIP archives. When historic claims conflict with the active v2.09 reruns, use the corrected current results. A historical PASS does not validate a newer model, and earlier research hypotheses are not silently approved as formal requirements.

## v1.93–v1.95: traceability of accepted recommendations and evidence layers

Work was classified as a proposal, mathematical specification, executable example, actual numeric test or full-system verification. Unit and authority conflicts must be repaired before integration. A paper closure is not validation of autonomous bodies or WORLD×MAG execution.

## v1.96–v1.98: reduced 24-DPB coupling and longer stability boundaries

Breathing, circulation, metabolism, heat regulation, renal-fluid and acid–base feedback were progressively specified. Successful 120-second or longer toy simulations did not validate healthy multiday operation. Simulation duration, supply boundary and parameter ranges had to be explicit.

## v1.99: 72-hour gas–renal–electrolyte research and geometry hypotheses

An exploratory 40-coordinate compartment model tracked water, Na/K/Cl and temperature across body forms. Forty coordinates do not mean forty biological mechanisms: some are controls, derived quantities, fixed parameters or audit values. A stimulus schedule that did not enact its reported eight-hour load became a permanent regression lesson.

Corrected stimulus boundaries, explicitly separated authoritative dynamic state from parameters, algebraic values, controls and audit counters, and specified candidate renal–circulatory–blood-gas coupling plus G-01–G-08 rules. The 40- and 28-field systems were **not** thereby fully integrated.

The historic 1–8h load lasts **seven**, not eight, hours.
