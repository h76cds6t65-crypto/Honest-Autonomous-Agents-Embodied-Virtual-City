We do **not** upload private conversations, personal identifiers, the confidential cumulative Word/ZIP archive, undisclosed memory records, unreviewed scripts with local paths, or character art / derivatives lacking redistribution permission. The research is documented through public equations and test conditions instead.

新文件提供**詳細而完整範圍的現行 v2.09 研究整理**，但不是 44 頁私人歷史 Word 的逐句無損翻譯，亦未聲稱公開原始 140MB 研究資料或提供 bit-for-bit 可重現的程式碼。原始程式／GLB 未公開，公開文字足以讓研究者獨立重建縮減方程與理解失敗條件。

The two languages target the same formulas, substantive technical findings, maturity boundaries and N01–N16 test statuses. Wording is a technical translation/re-rendering rather than a certified clause-by-clause mirror of the confidential original. Detailed text is a substantive open **documentation** publication; the private raw experimental inputs and source scripts are not being distributed here.
